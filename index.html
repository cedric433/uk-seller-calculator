UK Seller Profit Calculator

See what you actually keep on eBay, Etsy, Depop and Vinted after fees, postage and costs.

Rates used (checked 18 Oct 2026 from published sources, always confirm on each site before pricing):
  • eBay UK private sellers: no selling fees. Business: category % of item + postage, plus £0.30 per order (£0.40 over £10) and a 0.35% regulatory fee. Categories range from about 6.9% to 14.9%.
  • Etsy UK: £0.15 listing, 6.5% transaction, 4% + £0.20 payment processing, 0.48% regulatory fee (since 22 June 2026), optional 15% Offsite Ads.
  • Depop UK: 0% selling fee, 2.9% + £0.30 payment processing, optional 12% boost fee. Sources conflict on this, so verify.
  • Vinted: sellers pay no fees; buyers pay the protection fee.
  • Percentage fees are applied to item price plus postage charged. Estimates only, not tax or financial advice.
