Vinted UK Selling FAQs

Are there really no seller fees on Vinted?
Yes. Vinted is completely free for sellers in the UK. The platform makes its money by charging buyers a Buyer Protection fee (typically 3% to 8% plus a fixed £0.30 to £0.80) at checkout.

What extra costs should I watch out for?
While selling is free, your true profit is affected by the original cost of the item and any packaging materials you buy to ship it securely.

Are Item Bumps worth it?
Vinted offers optional paid features like 'Bumping' an item or 'Wardrobe Spotlight' to get more views. This calculator compares base profitability, but you should deduct bump costs from your final margin if you use them.
