Sources and assumptions

  • Percentage fees apply to item price plus postage charged. Margin = profit ÷ (item price + postage charged). Money is shown to the nearest penny, and target prices are rounded up to the next penny.
  • Highest profit only compares profit. It ignores demand, selling speed and whether you’re allowed to use that account type (eBay’s private rate is for casual selling, not trading).

eBay UK Selling FAQs

How much are eBay fees in the UK?
As of October 2024, private sellers pay no final value fees. Business sellers pay a category percentage (often around 11.9% for clothing) plus a fixed £0.30 per order (£0.40 if the order is over £10), and a 0.35% regulatory fee.

Do I pay fees on postage?
Yes, eBay calculates your percentage fee based on the total amount the buyer pays, which includes the item price and the postage you charge.

What about VAT?
eBay’s base fees are shown excluding VAT. If you are not a VAT-registered business, a 20% VAT charge is added to your fees.
