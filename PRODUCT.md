# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

UK marketplace sellers comparing where to list an item.

## Product Purpose

Compare estimated profit across eBay UK, Etsy UK, Depop, and Vinted after fees, postage, and costs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The calculator estimates platform fees and profit, supports seller cost and fee options, and saves recent calculations in the browser. Results are estimates; sellers should confirm current rates, eligibility, demand, and selling speed before deciding. This is not tax or financial advice.

## Evidence on Hand

The working calculator and its fee sources are in the repository. Do not invent endorsements, customer testimonials, or financial-performance claims.
